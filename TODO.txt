(D) 2026-10-30 IDEA: Appendix I: Priorities usage \

  ### Priorities

  The established priority axis description and usage guides are a bit thin, and broad.
  Normal, generic apps should easily go by on just 6 or 7 levels--not 26.

  Users may map those level to their preferred use.

  And for extended A-Z organisations other conventions can be established ad-hoc;
  organisations may provide controlled rankings;
  conventions can define pseudo-priorities, channels or facilities;
  and mapped to the above or to a more complex syntax.

  Cf. how Linux/BSD (log) event facility and priority are merged into a field PRI
  to channel messages.

(D) 2026-10-30 IDEA: High level jargon and structure / syntax

  An entry is spread over a header and possible follow up lines.

  The logical field sets of an record are a status or 'stat' set of
  fields, followed by a required "oneline" text or description that may be
  followed up more optional lines of free form text.

  Such oneline can be substructured, in the same way the category of TODO-entries
  defined here are; and such follow up text as well can be divided into sections
  or divisions of kinds. These are emergent properties, and the scope lifts
  / changes the focus for the rules but there is a clear, alternate and simple
  format structure.

  For simple parsing, the strict '(' `A-Z` ')' and 'x' prefix rules and lifecycle
  are a bit of a mixed bag.

  Alternatively, a related form could do away with any non-numeric character in
  the stat field set and restrict it to spaces and a few numerical helper characters.
  The parser can now scan a compact (but still ASCII) head, and use simple rules
  to get the stat and remaining textual record.
  Still for non numeric Ids and keys, external caching or further parsing is required.

  The oneline can be further split immediately as well, into a restricted
  left hand side and permissive right hand side.
  This yields the logical record Id, or user alias for it by taking in either the
  strict word or some pattern ending in ':' followed by a blank, EOL, or continuation.

  Furthermore embedding or (ASCII/unicode) harnassing can be more formally defined perhaps.

  Add document level metadata, in markdown-esque '---' surrounded YAML blocks.
